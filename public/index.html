Hush 🤫

Anonymous chat rooms. No login, no phone number. Messages are encrypted in your browser, so even the server can't read them.

  • 🔒 End-to-end encrypted (AES-256)
  • 📸 Photos you can download, 🎤 voice messages, 😊 emoji & reactions
  • ↩ Reply to messages & 🗑 Delete your messages for everyone
  • 🌙 Dark mode & ☀️ Light mode support
  • 🎭 Random nickname per room
  • ⏳ Rooms and messages vanish after 24 hours
  • 🔗 Join by link or QR code, share it where you trust people

Open source. The server can't read your chats as long as the code stays honest, so check it on GitHub.
